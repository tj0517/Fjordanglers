FAQ

Questions about fishing in Iceland

Answers come from the guides who run these trips.

Ask us directly
01 When is the fishing season in Iceland?

The guides' seasons run from April or May to September. Bergur Birgisson names May to July as best, Magnús Haukur April to July. Andri Fannberg's peak is June to August.

02 Is the fishing licence included?

It depends on the guide. Andri Fannberg's price includes the licence, and he handles river access. On Brynjar Arnarsson's trips the guiding fee is a flat rate and the licence is charged per person.

03 Do I need to bring my own gear?

Not with Brynjar Arnarsson: his guiding fee includes premium fly and spinning gear, and you are welcome to bring your own rod. Andri Fannberg hires out rods, reels, waders and wading boots for an extra fee.

04 Do guides pick me up in Reykjavík?

Andri Fannberg always includes pickup from central Reykjavík. Brynjar Arnarsson picks you up at your Reykjavík accommodation, around 09:00, in a 4x4.

05 Is a guided day in Iceland suitable for beginners?

Brynjar Arnarsson says yes: his guides read the water with you and coach you hands-on, although some rivers, such as the West Ranga, can be technically challenging.
