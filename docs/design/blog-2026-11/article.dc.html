Fishing in Norway 2026: Regulations, Licenses, and Limits

Licenses (fiskekort, fiskeravgift), export limits, gear disinfection, and what applies at sea, in lakes and salmon rivers.

Norway is an absolute paradise for anglers. From deep fjords and wild lakes to rushing rivers full of powerful salmon, the possibilities are endless. However, Norwegian law is very strict when it comes to nature conservation.

In short

  • Sea: no license needed from the shore or a boat.
  • Lakes and rivers: a fiskekort for the water you fish, plus ID.
  • Salmon rivers: national fiskeravgift, a local fiskekort and a disinfection certificate.
  • Export: 15 kg of marine fish per person, with a document from a registered camp.

Sea Angling in Norway: Free, but with Rules

Fishing in Norwegian fjords and the open sea is the most popular activity among tourists. Most importantly, it is very accessible.

Do you need a license for the sea?

No. A license is not required for recreational sea angling from the shore or a boat.

Good to know. You can fish the coastline and fjords outside protected zones, ports and military areas. Minimum sizes apply.

Freshwater Fishing: Lakes and Rivers

For lakes and rivers you need a license (fiskekort) for the water you fish, and ID. Local regulations apply on each water.

Salmon Rivers: Salmon and Sea Trout

You fish only designated river sections with a purchased license.

Important. Salmon rivers need the national fee (fiskeravgift), a local fiskekort and a disinfection certificate for your gear.

Exporting Caught Fish from Norway in 2026

15 kgmarine fish per person

For export you need a document from a registered camp.
